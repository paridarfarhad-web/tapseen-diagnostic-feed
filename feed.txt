The easiest reference to find in Grand Theft Auto VI related to Marvel is the RX-Men comic book. Throughout the game, there are dozens of magazines and comics for you to collect, including one about the RX-Men, which is clearly a parody of the X-Men. You can actually open and read the story, which turns the characters into pharmaceutical jokes. Dr. Placebo is the main villain, acting as a mix of Dr. Doom and Magneto. It all starts soon after Chapter 1, when, as Lucia, you find a flyer for a "pilot wanted" at the Starlet Motel's reception area. That will give you Kyle's number and you start doing side jobs for him involving chemicals. When you reach Chapter 3, you get a strange text from Kyle and investigate at his farm. Once there, you head to the back of the barn, which is surrounded by agents. Suddenly, Kyle bursts out as a chemically mutated creature who resembles the Red Hulk.

The moment was first famously found by streamer IShowSpeed and all of his viewers were just as shocked as he was. Kyle might not look exactly like Red Hulk, but he's massive, red, and ripping out of his clothes. The reference even goes far enough to show him throw someone into a helicopter, which is a scene pulled straight out of Captain America: Brave New World . You don't have to fight Kyle either, as you can just sit back and watch the carnage unfold from the side. Hopefully, there are even more Marvel references waiting to be found.
