But a new auction is giving fans the chance to own an original design for the character by effects master Phil Tippett and it is very different to the one we thankfully got.

Being sold by Heritage Auctions , this lot features a sculpted maquette of Groot's second form following his "death" in Guardians of the Galaxy . Instead of the cute and strangely cuddly plant-like being, Tippett's proposed design is captured in this piece is a grotesque "old man" Groot with almost pained expression and long spindly limbs. Although the character does have a Groot feel about it, it has a haunted quality that really would not have allowed retailers to stock up on cute cuddly toys and merchandise with its face on. The maquette is made of Magic Sculpt clay, painted in brown to make it look like it is made of wood. The piece is finished off by coming with its own terracotta plant pot with clay "soil" to make it looks like it is actually rooted into the container. The piece is certainly something that could be a talking point as part of any collection, and at the moment it is selling for what seems like a steal at $1,000. Whether it remains at that level is yet to be seen, but if so, then someone will get a great deal.

While Groot is usually involved in the comical side of the MCU, he is a character that has been involved in some of the most pivotal moments the Guardians have faced . In their first outing in 2014, Groot protects the team from being killed in the final act, enclosing the group in his branches and ultimately sacrificing himself for their survival. At the same time, this act prevented the Power Stone from falling into the hands of Ronan, which could have had consequences long before Thanos was able to finally acquire it before Infinity War . The result of Groot's sacrifice was a small shoot of his own being, which then would grow into Baby Groot by the second movie. Again, this form of Groot became the savior of the day, as his size allowed him to carry explosives to the core of Ego the living planet and destroy the megalomaniacal celestial being.

In Infinity War, a now teenage Groot was once again key in several moments, most prominently during the making of Thor's newly forged Stormbreaker by pulling together the pieces of the weapon and cutting off his own arm to create a handle for the weapon. Without the new ax, Thor would not have been able regain his powers and join the Avengers, even if their battle this time around ended in defeat to Thanos. Although it has not been revealed when the Guardians will return after their third solo outing, their future could depend on the events of Secret Wars . James Gunn previously worked on all Guardians stories and with his tenure as DC Studios CEO now over there is a chance he could return for a fourth movie, but there is just as much a chance that Marvel Studios will take the opportunity to prune the main Guardians group form the roster in Phase 7.

&nbsp; Share on X (Opens in new window) X Share on Facebook (Opens in new window) Facebook Add Us On Google Netflix is continuing to mine content from some of the most acclaimed cult-hit properties of the 1980s, 1990s and 2000s. After turning The Witcher and The Sandman into acclaimed hits, Netflix is taking on Grendel , the comic book series that author Matt Wagner first launched in 1982. Grendel has been an unusual icon in comics: the publishing rights have taken several sharp turns, while the sporadic nature of the character's books and lore is a puzzle not every fan has put together. So what is Netflix doing now that so many other failed adaptations couldn't?

Grendel will be adapted as an animated series by studio Bobbypills, which produces DC's Creature Commandos , with Bobak Esfarjani ( Alien: Earth, Widow's Bay ) acting as showrunner, writer, and executive producer. Now Netflix is releasing full details on the series and who is invovled, which you can read below.

“ Grendel has been at the forefront of independent comics for over forty years, and we’re thrilled to bring Matt Wagner’s creation to Netflix,” John Derderian, Vice President, Animation Series + Kids & Family TV at Netflix, said in a statement. “Like the comic, this animated series will break the traditional superhero mold just as Matt's time-honored comic book has always done. Bobak, Dark Horse Entertainment and Bobbypills are bold storytellers who love this world as much as its fans do, and we’re so excited to partner with them to bring this story to screen.”

Dark Horse Comics was the publisher that picked up Grendel and relaunched it in 1992 after the comic's original publisher, Comico, went bankrupt at the end of the '80s. Chris Tongue, Tim Wiesch, and Jay Komas of Dark Horse Entertainment will be attached as executive producers, alongside Matt Wagner and Lydia Antonini.

"It’s incredibly exciting to see my creation finally make this leap to the animated screen!" Matt Wagner wrote in his statment. "Netflix’s dynamic new series retains the source material’s dark adult themes and also brings a fresh and thrilling approach to the narrative. This is Grendel as I’ve always wanted to see the character portrayed—a hyper-kinetic level of action, bracing explosions of violence and a poignant engaging storyline.”

Grendel has followed several characters who have taken up the titular mantle and been tragically influenced by it. It started with Hunter Rose, an author who moonlights as a masked assassin and eventually becomes the head of New York City's organized crime world.

The Netflix series follows the framework of the original character and story. As per the show's logline: "After the woman he loves is shockingly murdered, Hunter Rose devotes himself to one thing: vengeance. But as he tears through street gangs, the elite, and New York City’s supernatural underworld in pursuit of her killer, a chance at a real life forces him to choose: walk away from ultimate power, or become the very monster he hunts."

Grendel is in development at Netflix. No release date has been set yet.
