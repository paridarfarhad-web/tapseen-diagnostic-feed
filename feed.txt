The world of Diablo 5 is said to be a version of Sanctuary that has been set ablaze and destroyed by Diablo himself, turning the land into a hellish wasteland. This dystopian version of the world lends itself to the Plague Knight or Demon Hunter classes, but the Crusader or Paladin would be welcome saviors in such a setting too. From various Diablo games, Crusaders and Paladins have extremely similar playstyles, being defensive tanks with hard-hitting abilities powered by holy magic. There are some subtle differences, but for all intents and purposes, players would be eager to see either of these classes make an appearance in another Diablo .

Having a Paladin or Crusader class be part of the base roster for Diablo 5 would be a new shift for the archetypes too, as they tend to be inclusions much later in a Diablo game's life cycle. For example, the Crusader was a DLC class for Diablo 3 , while Diablo 4 didn't add the Paladin until a big post-launch expansion. Crusaders are masters of Shields and holy magic to stun foes, using a more defensive play style, while Paladins are more aggressive, with heavier weapons to deal high damage to single targets. Players might have their preferences, but perhaps the Paladin's recent existence in Diablo 4 makes the Crusader more likely to appear in the next game.

Currently, the roster for Diablo 5 's classes doesn't have a dedicated archetype tied to magic, even if the Plague Knight will have some supernatural powers over pestilence. While the Wizard, Druid, or Witch Doctor could all be valid classes to revive for the game, I'd argue that the Sorcerer is the most iconic one you almost can't have a Diablo title without. The Sorcerer is a class that has existed in the series since the very first Diablo , representing a key type of character players can create. Out of all the classes that could return, the Sorcerer is organized perhaps the best to fit whatever RPG systems change going into Diablo 5 .

The Sorcerer has powers over Fire, Cold, and Lightning, marking a study into elemental magics for maximum DPS rather than the Wizard's versatility or the Necromancer's AoE spells. Having these studies of magic gives players three paths to go down when making a Sorcerer character, leading to an open door of possibilities when it comes to builds. Players can typically invest completely into something like Fire, tapping into the benefits of burn damage and AoE effects, or try to mix spells together to deal with different situations. One Sorcerer may rely on high mobility through an investment in Lightning and Cold, while another dips into Fire and Lightning for raw damage potential or crowd control.

There are many ways to build a Sorcerer in every Diablo , but the class is also a good pick due to how their variety also extends to gear.
