The Justice League is DC's greatest team . The group first debuted back in 1960, bringing together the greatest heroes of DC Comics at the time – Superman, Batman, Wonder Woman, Green Lantern, the Flash, Aquaman, and Martian Manhunter. However, sidekicks didn't get to join the team, which would lead Robin to start his own group, the Teen Titans. He became Nightwing with the Titans and would lead the adult versions of the group. Justice League: Cry for Justice began this new era, but fans were turned off almost immediately. It started off the run on the worst possible foot and no one has tried to make Nightwing a member of the League since.

Bruce Wayne watched his parents bleed out on the cold concrete of Crime Alley, changing his life forever, leading to him becoming Batman. Wayne went to the circus years later when it came to Gotham and watched as the Flying Graysons were gunned down under the big top, their son forced to watch his parents die. Dick and Bruce were suddenly inextricably linked, leading to the Dynamic Duo becoming the protectors of Gotham City. Batman trained Robin to be the best of the best and he learned his lessons well, becoming one of the most formidable heroes out there.

Nightwing is an amazing vigilante. He's one of the best fighters around, a great leader, a smart detective and strategist, someone that everyone looks up to. However, he's just not on the same level as the Dark Knight. Batman can invent just about anything and is used to dealing with the biggest threats in the multiverse. Dick rarely gets to take the lead on situations like that and he doesn't have the scientific know-how of his mentor. He's great at what he does, but he'll never actually reach Batman's level.
