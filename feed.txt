In this image alone, H.E.R.B.I.E. is walking alongside Yelena Belova, and they will almost certainly be a hilarious pair. This also bodes well regarding the fan concern that Doomsday was going to struggle to bring these various groups together, as Avengers: Infinity War had years and multiple projects to build up to that, and Doomsday will, seemingly, be bringing these universes together with minimal preamble. Hopefully, though, this image is a sign that the groups will mesh just fine. Now, it's just waiting to see how audiences react to this H.E.R.B.I.E. change.
