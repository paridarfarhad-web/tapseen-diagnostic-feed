This has not been the best season of The Traitors . In fact, you could easily say this has been a below-average season. Fans on social media and those I have talked to in real life seem to all be in agreement on that. There’s a growing consensus that the show needs to change. The biggest, loudest and most obvious change we’ve gotten this season has been the casting of non-celebrities. The US version of The Traitors has historically relied on reality television personalities to provide the drama and bring their fanbases. This season, NBC decided to move the property from Peacock to network television and cast it like a more traditional competition show with randoms . It would be easy to just look at that decision and blame all the problems on the newbies, but that’s actually not the right takeaway.

The biggest problem by a very large margin with Traitors: New Blood is the episode runtimes. The show simply does not work in an hour-long time slot on NBC. In theory, the episodes on Peacock are the same length, as they’re typically between an hour and an hour and ten minutes. Because they’re on streaming, however, that runtime is the actual length of each episode. On network television, an hour-long time slot is actually forty-two minutes or so of real content, which isn’t even close to the amount of time the players need to complete one cycle of the game.

That means every single episode, the editors are making an impossible choice. They can either not include one or more major elements of the show (the breakfast, the mission, the roundtable and the turret), they can completely remove all of the strategy scenes that happen between those major elements, or they can drop most of the confessionals where the players talk directly to the camera and tell us their motivations and theories. There is simply no way to get through all of that in forty-two minutes. So, that has left every episode feeling either incomplete because it didn’t make it through a full cycle of the game, or feeling like a highlight video that simply recaps the basics of what happened.

That format and structure are the problem. The runtime is the problem. It’s not the cast. Even with less confessionals and less screentime, we’ve gotten good content from these players. I’m not saying the Traitors: New Blood cast is better than the cast on Big Brother 14 or Survivor: Heroes vs Villains , but there are some breakout personalities here and plenty of likeable, solid characters who have popped and would have looked even better with more of a chance to talk to the camera.
