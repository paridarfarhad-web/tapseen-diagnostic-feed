It's understandable why Galadriel and Sauron's connection bothers so many people. It's all part of character development: they evolve step by step into the figures we eventually meet in the films. They're not the same people, with the same mindset and perspective throughout their entire lives, and if some fans kept that in mind, they might find it easier to enjoy the series.

In the end, maybe a big part of the audience is too focused on Sauron and Galadriel (and now Celeborn) as a potential romantic storyline, judging the show because of that, when it would be much more interesting to look at the bigger role their dynamic plays in the overall story. They're growing as individual characters, but their interactions also bring out some of the most important matters in Tolkien's universe . You don't have to agree with the series' approach, but seeing it from this perspective could make The Rings of Power more enjoyable.
