People are discovering that some “iconic” features from past games, but are finding plenty of new features, challenges, and even some surprises — including a hilarious X-Men parody that pretty much no one would have expected that is not only funny, but honestly kind of holds up.

As users have shared on line, there are more than 30 various magazines and comic books located in GTA 6 , including the RX-Men comic book. The comic, published by the in-world fictional ED Comics, is priced at $1.25 and is available for purchase at the 24/7 store in Key Lento. But while the in-game book is an interesting bit of fun-poking at real world elements like the popularity of superheroes and the upcoming Avengers: Doomsday , what’s especially interesting about RX-Men is how it satirizes the iconic heroes and turns them into a much bigger joke.

A ‘X-MEN’ parody exists within GTA 6. (📸: @dzakidj ) pic.twitter.com/ziWH31W2hW

While most Marvel Comics fans are familiar with the Merry Mutants and their wide range of powers and sometimes unique physical appearances thanks to decades of comics as well as movies, GTA 6 twists the idea into a pharmaceutical satire.&nbsp; The cover features a very Magneto-esque figure named Dr. Placebo who the book reveals “seeks to free the world of pharmaceuticals” with his “army of alt medicine minions”. There are also the heroes with Capsule, Side Effect, Diagnosis, and Mr. I, the latter of which is a play on MRI.

The satire here is what’s fascinating. The RX-Men appear to represent, essentially, better living through chemistry or in this case, medication and medical care while Dr. Placebo represents people who think those things are a scam at best and toxic at worst — there’s an image where a devotee of Dr. Placebo tells his worried partner who tells him to take his medication or he’ll die to shut up and give him raw milk instead while another devotee has just one month to live and is trusting Dr. Placebo’s crystals rather than medical care. It’s a clear bit of satire about the alternative healthcare movement, but that also doesn’t mean the RX-Men are free of satirization. Side Effect — which appears to be a satire of Beast — has a laundry list of serious medical conditions that makes him pretty much useless leading to the RX-Men’s Jean Grey, Diagnosis to step in instead.

Of course, RX-Men isn’t the only book you can find in the GTA 6 world of comics. Another ED Comic you can find in the game is Ratface, which is very clearly a parody of DC Comics’ Batman. Of course, here, RX-Men and Ratface are from the same publisher so who knows, maybe GTA 6 has the Marvel/DC crossover we crave.

The Young Griff subplot is one of the biggest mysteries left in the novels. As the show went on and avoided that story, Varys became less cunning and far less interesting.

This may be a controversial pick because Robb Stark feels more prominent in the show. In the novels, he and Rickon are the only Starks who aren't POV characters, so Robb's story is seen through the eyes of Catelyn. In Game of Thrones , we watch plenty of scenes from his rise as the King in the North to his untimely death at the Red Wedding. In the books, Robb is comforted by Jeyne Westerling after learning of his brothers' supposed deaths. They sleep together and Robb marries her to protect her honor, while in the show, Robb simply falls for Talisa and throws everything away to marry her. Without that honor aspect, Robb is just someone who made a bad decision with his heart, which is far less intriguing.

A Feast for Crows is often seen as the worst book in A Song of Ice and Fire , yet it does shine a light on the Martells and the Grejoys. That means a lot of time is given to Euron Greyjoy, who is one of the scariest characters in the franchise. He's someone who is incredibly ambitious while also being increasingly dangerous. He basically wants to become a god, steal a dragon, and is willing to dabble in the dark arts to get what he wants.

Euron's standing heading into The Winds of Winter is one of the most interesting elements of the series. He could change the face of Westeros and the battle for the Iron Throne entirely. Unfortunately, Game of Thrones changed him into a generic, campy villain. The TV show counterpart doesn't seem nearly as threatening and his ambition often comes down to wanting to have sex with the Queen. Nothing captivating about Euron remained and what we got was a hollow shell of someone who could've changed the game in the final seasons.

It might be kind of a cop-out to group all of Dorne together but Game of Thrones messed them all up so badly that it's hard not to. In the books, Doran Martell is a cunning man who is playing the long game in his desire for revenge against the Lannisters, to the point where almost nobody knows what he's planning. The strategic elements of his story are some of the highlights of the last few books. The show just plays on the idea that he's weak, which he's mostly feigning in the novels, and doesn't do anything beyond that, making him a passive character.

The Sand Snakes are ruined in the show by becoming interchangable. They give no time to differentiate Oberyn's daughters, so nobody cares about them. In the books, each has their own nuanced personality, are diverse, and each is carrying out a plot from Doran , adding so much to the Dorne storylines. The show also entirely cut Doran's heir Ariane Martell, who had her own great plans, and basically melded some of those ideas into Ellaria and the Sand Snakes. What should've been a gripping political subplot was reduced to nothing of interest and viewers often groaned when a Dorne scene came on TV, which isn't how it should've been.
