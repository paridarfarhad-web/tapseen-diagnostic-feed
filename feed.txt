Video podcasts are coming to Apple TV, as the tech giant links together its Apple Podcasts and Apple TV app in a unique setup that only it could deliver.

The change will bring video shows from Apple Podcasts to the Apple TV app, with no subscription required. Notably, users will be able to move seamlessly between the apps, letting them pick up where they left off regardless of which app or which device they were watching on.

Apple says that in the last three months the number of podcasts publishing video to its platform has doubled, while the video episode catalogue has tripled. The company says that top podcasts that had video episodes saw a 20 percent viewing boost, on average.

The Apple TV news means that video podcast creators now have significantly more reach than they had exclusively through the podcast app, particularly on TV sets where the Apple TV app is more likely to live.

It also gives Apple TV a significantly larger pool of video content, helping it more effectively compete in a crowded video app ecosystem. YouTube has become a dominant podcast platform by leaning into video, while other premium video platforms like Netflix and Disney+ have sought to acquire video podcasts to supplement their other content.

Apple, of course, is already a power player in the podcast space, now it is just using that muscle to help bolster other parts of its ecosystem.
