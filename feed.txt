However, for Stewart’s whole career, he had wanted to do comedy. He was rarely cast in comedy roles. During his career with the Royal Shakespeare Company, he was cast as Touchstone, the fool of Duke Frederick, for the 1968 production of As You Like It. Touchstone is typically a comedy role, given that he’s a jester. The director for As You Like It had a different take on the character, however, envisioning him as a sad, old man. Still, through collaborations with Seth MacFarlane , known for the television shows Family Guy and American Dad , and Ricky Gervais, audiences saw another side of Patrick Stewart — a comedic one. Stewart’s wit could be used for humor, and although the roles were never quite as popular as Picard and Xavier, they deserve to be part of Patrick Stewart’s legacy.

Patrick Stewart often credited Seth MacFarlane with having the greatest impact on his career regarding comedy. MacFarlane helped people see that there was another actor inside Patrick Stewart. Since his days at the Royal Shakespeare Company, other actors loved saying lines in Stewart’s voice, finding amusement, but they didn’t find Stewart himself funny.

Stewart had recurring voice roles in MacFarlane projects such as Family Guy and American Dad. For American Dad , Stewart voiced the inner thoughts of Baby Susie Swanson. Baby Susie was too young to speak, with her dialogue on the show mainly being noises voiced by Christina Milian. However, the show sometimes highlighted her inner thoughts. Not only were they in Patrick Stewart’s voice, but Stewart broke the fourth wall, directly asking the audience, &#8220;How are you liking the program so far?” In American Dad , Stewart wasn’t working against his voice, but rather it was working for him. Blunt Talk featured Stewart as Walter Blunt — who shared the name of Stewart’s first role with the Royal Shakespeare Company, Sir Walter Blunt of Henry IV, Part I — who was a British newscaster who moved to LA. Blunt’s main ambition through the series was to conquer American nightly news, but he quickly found out that his goal was harder to achieve than initially thought.
